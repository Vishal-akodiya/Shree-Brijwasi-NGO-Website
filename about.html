Who We Are

Shree Brijwasi Jatav Samaj Sewa Samiti is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to uplifting marginalized communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development. Founded with a vision to create an equitable society, we have been working tirelessly to bridge the gap between privilege and poverty.

Our comprehensive programs span across education scholarships, medical camps, skill development workshops, women empowerment initiatives, and environmental conservation projects. We believe in the power of collective action and community-driven change.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through quality education, accessible healthcare, and sustainable livelihood opportunities, ensuring dignity and equality for all. We strive to create an inclusive society where every individual can realize their full potential.

Our Vision

A just and equitable society where every individual, regardless of their background, has access to education, healthcare, and opportunities for growth. We envision communities that are self-reliant, empowered, and living with dignity.
